# IceCream Demo Outline for the Final Pitch

## 1. Opening Problem

Campus artists share work in fast-moving chats and general feeds where posts disappear, multi-page projects lose their order, and feedback is often shallow.

## 2. Target User

Campus digital artists, illustrators, comic creators, fandom artists, and art-club communities.

## 3. Product Solution

IceCream is a focused artwork community prototype with alias accounts, multi-image upload, a current/recommended gallery, partial-title and tag search, artwork detail, likes, profiles, and creator summaries.

## 4. Demo Flow

1. Landing page and “How to use”
2. Gallery and current artwork tags
3. Alias sign-up and login
4. Multi-image artwork upload
5. Title/tag search
6. Artwork detail and like
7. Creator profile, dashboard, and logout
8. Honest statement of critique/moderation gaps

## 5. Evidence

- **Customer discovery:** work gets buried in active chats; users want categories and tags.
- **Validation:** 17/20 tasks completed; average usefulness 4.4/5; 16/20 would use again.
- **Current smoke test:** eight key pages available; tag search and artwork detail return stored data.
- **Next priorities:** final moderated testing, clearer content-level wording, mobile usability, and presentation polish.

## 6. Closing Value Message

IceCream helps campus artists keep multi-image work organized, searchable, and connected to a visible creator identity. Final improvements will make feedback more structured and the community safer.
