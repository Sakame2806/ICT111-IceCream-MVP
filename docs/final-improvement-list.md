## Priority Definition

- **Important:** should be completed to make the final prototype clear and testable.
- **Useful:** improves usability or presentation quality but does not block the central demo.
- **Future:** optional work outside the current MVP scope.

## Non-Necessary Features

Structured critique, critique-status badges, moderator tools, and other speculative features are not part of the current Lab 14 implementation plan. They are excluded from the improvement backlog and do not block the final prototype.

## Lab 14 Release Gate

The prototype is ready for the final pitch when:

1. The planned moderated test is completed and documented.
2. Content-level wording and major mobile usability issues are reviewed.
3. Demo screenshots match the current working build.
4. The team rehearses the final demo using `docs/demo-script.md`.
