# Digital Go-to-Market Plan

## Product / Prototype Name
IceCream

## Target Early Users
Campus digital artists, illustrators, and hobbyist creators looking for structured critique to improve their skills and connect with peers sharing similar styles or fandom interests.

## Main Value Proposition
A dedicated community platform for campus artists to share artwork, discover peers, and receive structured, constructive critiques to improve their skills.

## Channel Plan

| Channel | Target User | Message Angle | User Action Expected | Metric to Track | Why This Channel Fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Student Art Clubs | Campus artists | Get structured feedback and grow together | Click beta link | Link clicks / Sign-ups | Target demographic already gathers here to discuss art |
| Instagram & TikTok | Digital artists | Stop settling for shallow comments, get real critiques | Visit landing page | Profile link clicks / Sign-ups | Visual platforms are the primary portfolio tools for artists |
| Class Chats (Design/Art) | Art/Design students | Organize art and feedback better than a busy chat | Click demo link | Link clicks / Demo views | Highlights the pain point of cluttered chat histories |

## Launch Sequence
1. Prepare landing page and demo link.
2. Share to selected early-user channel.
3. Track response using spreadsheet, form, or prototype logs.
4. Collect feedback.
5. Revise message, CTA, or prototype feature if needed.

## Risk and Mitigation
- **Risk:** People might be hesitant to upload their art for critique due to fear of negative feedback.
  - **Mitigation:** Emphasize "constructive and structured feedback" in all marketing messages and provide clear community guidelines on the landing page to ensure a safe environment.
- **Risk:** Low conversion rate from Instagram/TikTok because users don't want to leave the app.
  - **Mitigation:** Ensure the landing page is mobile-optimized and clearly states the immediate value of joining the waitlist in just a few seconds.
