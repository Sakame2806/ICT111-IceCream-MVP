# IceCream Landing Page Content

## Target User

IceCream is designed for campus digital artists, student illustrators, comic
artists, creative hobbyists, and art-club communities that need a focused place
to publish and discover visual work.

## One-Sentence Value Proposition

IceCream gives campus artists a focused home where their work remains organized,
searchable, and visible beyond a fast-moving chat feed.

## Headline

**Your art deserves more than a fast-moving feed.**

## Problem Statement

Artwork shared in general chat groups and social feeds is quickly buried by new
messages. In the Lab 03 discovery interviews, 6 of 10 participants reported
that artwork disappears quickly in active chats, while 8 of 10 said categories
and tags would improve discovery. Existing channels also tend to produce brief
emoji reactions instead of focused creative discussion.

## Solution

IceCream is a web prototype for publishing and discovering campus artwork.
Artists can create an alias-based account, upload single- or multi-image work,
add titles and tags, and keep their uploads together on a personal profile.
Visitors can browse recent work, search through partial title or tag matches,
view multi-image work in order, and express interest with likes.

## Demonstrable Features

1. **Artwork publishing:** Upload up to five JPEG, PNG, or GIF images with a
   title, caption, tags, and content level.
2. **Title and tag search:** Search published work by partial title matches,
   partial tag matches, or both.
3. **Multi-image presentation:** Display every image from top to bottom in the
   artist's uploaded order.
4. **Artist profiles:** Show all non-deleted work belonging to the signed-in
   artist.
5. **Likes and tags:** Like or unlike artwork and allow the artwork owner to add
   tags.

Comments are intentionally described as a future iteration because comment
submission is not yet implemented.

## Calls to Action

- Primary: **Explore the gallery**
- Secondary: **Create an account**
- Supporting: **Open upload**, **Try search**, **Join the test**

All CTA links connect to working prototype pages.

## Validation Message

The 20-participant MVP test produced an 85% task-completion rate, an average
usefulness score of 4.40/5, and 80% return interest. The 65% confusion rate is
also shown because it identifies the next improvement priority: clearer
navigation and first-time guidance.

## Responsible Data Statement

IceCream uses aliases rather than real names or student IDs. Passwords are
stored as salted hashes in the local prototype. Users should upload only work
they have the right to share, and submitted files are used for educational
prototype demonstration.
